How it works

  1. Register

    Create a free account and log in.

  2. Pick a service

    Choose the counter you need.

  3. Get a token

    Receive a numbered token and see your position.

  4. Get called

    Staff call the next token. Your status updates automatically.

For students and staff

Live queue position, an honest waiting-time estimate, token history and one-tap cancellation.

For administrators

Call the next token, mark it serving or completed, manage counters and view statistics.

Fair and safe

Tokens are strictly sequential per service and day. Two admins can never call the same token.
